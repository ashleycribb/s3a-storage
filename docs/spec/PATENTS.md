# S3A Patent Non-Assertion Pledge

## 1. Non-Assertion Pledge

The contributors and authors of the **S3A Hyper-Tile Storage Specification** ("Specification") hereby irrevocably covenant and pledge that they will not assert any Patent Claims against any individual, corporation, or entity ("Implementer") for making, having made, using, selling, offering to sell, importing, or otherwise distributing any software, firmware, hardware, or implementation that conforms to the Specification ("Compliant Implementation").

## 2. Definitions

- **"Patent Claims"** means any and all claims of patents and patent applications, anywhere in the world, currently owned or later acquired by the pledgors, that are necessarily infringed by the implementation of the mandatory or optional portions of the Specification, where such infringement cannot be avoided while remaining compliant with the Specification.
- **"Compliant Implementation"** means any product, library, software system, tool, or hardware device that implements the binary wire format, data structures, CRC32C validation, dual-slot atomic commit protocol, hyper-tile layouts, or query interfaces defined in the Specification.

## 3. Scope and Conditions

1. **Royalty-Free & Worldwide**: This non-assertion pledge is perpetual, irrevocable (subject to Section 4), worldwide, royalty-free, and fully transferable.
2. **Open Implementations**: This pledge applies equally to proprietary, commercial, permissive open-source, and copyleft implementations. No license fees, royalties, or registration requirements shall ever be demanded for complying with the Specification.
3. **No Sublicense Required**: Downstream users, distributors, and deployers of Compliant Implementations receive the full benefit of this non-assertion pledge directly from the pledgors.

## 4. Defensive Termination (Reciprocity)

This pledge shall terminate immediately as to a specific Implementer if that Implementer (or any affiliate controlling, controlled by, or under common control with that Implementer) files, maintains, or sponsors any patent infringement litigation, claim, or proceeding asserting that:
1. Any Compliant Implementation of the Specification infringes any patent claim owned or controlled by that Implementer; or
2. The Specification itself infringes any patent claim owned or controlled by that Implementer.

## 5. Continuity and Successors

This pledge is binding upon all successors, assigns, trustees, and legal representatives of the pledgors. Any transfer of ownership or grant of exclusive license in any Patent Claims subject to this pledge shall be made expressly subject to the non-assertion obligations set forth herein.
